Honeypot Trap Generator

Generate a hidden form field that real users never fill in but simple spam bots often do.

Settings

How to Use It

Add this field to your form, and on your server, reject any submission where this field is not empty. Real users never see or fill it because it is visually hidden, but basic bots that auto-fill every input on a page will populate it, giving you an easy way to silently discard their submission.

This catches unsophisticated bots only. Combine it with rate limiting or a captcha if you are getting targeted spam from more advanced scripts.

Client-side tool. Nothing you enter here is stored or sent anywhere.
